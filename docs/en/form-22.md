### Instructions for using Form No. 22-VDS

**(1)** State the name of the People's Court issuing the decision; if it is a district-level People's Court, urban district People's Court, town People's Court, or provincial city People's Court, state clearly which district-level People's Court, urban district People's Court, town People's Court, or provincial city People's Court (for example: People's Court of Thường Tín District, Hanoi City); if it is a provincial-level People's Court or centrally governed city People's Court, state that provincial (city) People's Court (for example: People's Court of Hà Nam Province).

**(2)** The first box states the number, the second box states the year, the third box states the symbol of the type of decision (for example: "No.: 20/2018/QĐST-KDTM".

**(3)** State the type of civil matter that the People's Court has docketed for resolution.

**(4)** If the civil matter is resolved by one Judge, this content need not be stated.

**(5)** State the full name and title of the Hearing clerk.

**(6)** State the place where the hearing took place (for example: headquarters of People's Court of Ý Yên District, Nam Định Province).

**(7)** State the number, symbol, date of docketing of the civil matter and the type of civil matter that the People's Court has docketed for resolution.

**(8)** If the petitioner is an individual, state clearly the full name, address of place of residence and place of work (if any) of that person; if a minor, after the name additionally state the date, month, and year of birth of that person; if an agency or organization, state the name and headquarters address of that agency or organization.

**(9)** State only when there is a legal representative of the petitioner and state the full name, address of place of residence and place of work (if any) of that person; state clearly whether the person is a legal representative or authorized representative of the petitioner; if a legal representative, note in parentheses the relationship between that person and the petitioner; if an authorized representative, note in parentheses "authorization document dated … month … year ….."
Example 1: Mr. Nguyễn Văn A, residing at…………… is the legal representative of the petitioner (General Director of Tân Phước Thịnh Construction Joint Stock Company).
Example 2: Ms. Lê Thị B, residing at ……………..is the authorized representative of the petitioner (Authorization document dated….. month…. year….).

**(10)** State only when there is a guardian of lawful rights and interests of the petitioner and state the full name, address of place of residence and place of work (if any) of that person (if a lawyer, state which Law Firm the lawyer belongs to and which Bar Association); if there are multiple petitioners, state specifically for which petitioner lawful rights and interests are protected.

**(11)** State similarly as guided at point (8).

**(12)** State similarly as guided at point (9).

**(13)** State similarly as guided at point (10).

**(14)** State the full name and address of place of residence and place of work (if any) of the witness.

**(15)** State the full name and address of place of work of the interpreter; if there is no place of work, state the address of place of residence.

**(16)** State the full name and address of place of work of the Expert Examiner; if there is no place of work, state the address of place of residence.

**(17)** State clearly the contents that the petitioner requested the People's Court to resolve.

**(18)** State the findings of the People's Court and the bases for accepting or not accepting the request. In this section, paragraphs are numbered in [] brackets.

**(19)** State clearly the corresponding point, clause, and article of the Civil Procedure Code and related laws to issue the decision.

**(20)** State the People's Court's decisions on resolution of the civil matter.

**(21)** State clearly the persons who must pay the first-instance court fee for resolution of civil matter and the court fee amount each person must pay; if exempt from payment (or entitled to exemption or reduction) of the first-instance court fee for resolution of civil matter, state clearly that payment is not required (or exemption or reduction of the first-instance court fee for resolution of civil matter is granted).

**(22)** State the People's Court's decision on the right to appeal and protest of the party and the People's Procuracy.

**(23)** State only the right to request enforcement, obligation to enforce, and statute of limitations for request for enforcement in accordance with the Law on Civil Judgment Enforcement for decisions enforceable pursuant to the provisions at Article 482 of the Civil Procedure Code.
