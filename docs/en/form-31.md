### Instructions for using Form No. 31-VDS

**(1)** Enter the name of the Court issuing the decision; if it is a district, urban district, town, or provincial city People's Court, specify the name of that district, urban district, town, or provincial city People's Court (for example: People's Court of Thuong Tin District, Hanoi City); if it is a provincial or centrally run city-level People's Court, enter that provincial (city) People's Court (for example: People's Court of Ha Nam Province).

**(2)** The first box records the number and the second box records the year of issuance of the Decision.

**(3) and (4)** Enter the corresponding point, clause, and article of the Civil Procedure Code and the Law on Marriage and Family.

**(5)** The first box records the number and the second box records the year of docketing of the marriage and family matter.

**(6)** Clearly enter the full name and address of residence of the petitioner;

**(7)** If an individual, clearly enter the full name and address of residence and place of work (if any) of that person; if an agency or organization, enter the name and headquarters address of that agency or organization.

**(8)** Enter the findings of the Court on the contents agreed by the Parties as recorded in the Minutes of unsuccessful reconciliation mediation. In this section, paragraphs are numbered in order within [].
