### Instructions for using Form No. 11-VDS

**(1)** State the name of the Court issuing the notice; if it is a district-level People's Court, provincial city People's Court, or town People's Court of a province or centrally run city, clearly state which district-level, provincial city, or town People's Court of which province or centrally run city (for example: Thuong Tin District People's Court, Hanoi City); if it is a provincial or centrally run city People's Court, state that provincial (city) People's Court (for example: Ha Nam Provincial People's Court).

**(2) and (3)** If the person required to supplement documents and evidence is an individual, state full name and address of place of residence and place of work (if any) of that person; if an agency or organization, state the name and headquarters address of that agency or organization. If sent by a representative by operation of law, after the full name write "- is the representative by operation of law of the person with the right to request" and clearly state the full name of the person with the right to request; if sent by an authorized representative, write "- is the authorized representative of the person with the right to request under the power of attorney established on…….." and clearly state the full name of the person with the right to request.

**(4)** State the number, symbol, day, month, year of docketing of the civil matter and the type of civil matter that the Court dockets for resolution.

**(5) and (7)** If an individual, write Mr. or Ms. without stating the full name; if an agency or organization, state the name of that agency or organization.

**(6)** State specifically the documents and evidence to be supplemented.
