### Instructions for using Form No. 02-VDS:
**(1) and (5)** State the type of civil matter that the person withdrawing the petition had requested the Court to resolve in accordance with the provisions of the Civil Procedure Code (for example: "Request to declare a person missing"; "Request to annul a resolution of the General Meeting of Shareholders"; "Request to declare a labor contract void").

**(2)** If it is a district-level People's Court, provincial city People's Court, or town People's Court, clearly state which district-level, provincial city, or town People's Court of which province or centrally run city (for example: Thuong Tin District People's Court, Hanoi City); if it is a provincial or centrally run city People's Court, state that provincial (city) People's Court (for example: Ha Nam Provincial People's Court).

**(3)** If the person withdrawing the petition is an individual, clearly state full name, date of birth, and national identity card/citizen identification card/passport number or other identity document of that person; if it is an agency or organization, state the name of the agency or organization and the full name of the legal representative of that agency or organization. If it is a representative by operation of law, after the full name write "- is the representative by operation of law of the person with the right to request" and clearly state the full name of the person with the right to request; if it is an authorized representative, write "- is the authorized representative of the person with the right to request under the power of attorney established on…….." and clearly state the full name of the person with the right to request. Where multiple persons jointly withdraw the petition, number them 1, 2, 3,... and provide full information for each person.

**(4)** If the person withdrawing the petition is an individual, state the full address of place of residence and place of work (if any) of that person at the time of withdrawing the petition (for example: Binh An Hamlet, Phu Cuong Commune, My Duc District, Hanoi City); if it is an agency or organization, state the headquarters address of that agency or organization at the time of withdrawing the petition (for example: headquarters at No. 20 Ly Thuong Kiet Street, Hoan Kiem District, Hanoi City).

**(6)** State specifically the reason for requesting to withdraw the petition (for example: the person requested to be declared missing has returned,…..).

**(7)** Where the person withdrawing the petition withdraws part of the request, clearly state the contents of the part of the request being withdrawn.

**(8)** If the person withdrawing the petition is an individual, that person's signature or fingerprint is required; if it is an agency or organization, the legal representative of the agency or organization must sign, state full name and position, and affix the seal of that agency or organization. Where the person withdrawing the petition is an enterprise, the use of the seal shall comply with the provisions of the Law on Enterprises. Where multiple persons jointly withdraw the request, all shall sign and clearly state the full name of each person at the end of the petition.
