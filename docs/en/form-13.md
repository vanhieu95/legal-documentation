### Instructions for using Form No. 13-VDS

**(1)** State the name of the People's Court issuing the summons; if it is a district-level People's Court, urban district People's Court, town People's Court, or provincial city People's Court, state clearly which district-level People's Court, urban district People's Court, town People's Court, or provincial city People's Court (for example: People's Court of Thường Tín District, Hanoi City); if it is a provincial-level People's Court or centrally governed city People's Court, state that provincial (city) People's Court (for example: People's Court of Hà Nam Province); if it is a High People's Court, state clearly the High People's Court at (Hanoi, Da Nang, Ho Chi Minh City).

**(2)** The first box states the number, the second box states the year of the summons.

**(3)** State the full name and address of the individual summoned by the People's Court.

**(4)** State the number, symbol, date of docketing of the civil matter and the type of civil matter that the People's Court has docketed for resolution.

**(5)** State specifically the place of work with the witness (for example: Room 201, Building A, headquarters of Ba Đình District People's Court, No. 53 Linh Lang Street, Ba Đình District, Hanoi City).

**(6)** Depending on each case, state specifically the contents that the People's Court needs to clarify.

**(7), (8)** State the name of the witness.
