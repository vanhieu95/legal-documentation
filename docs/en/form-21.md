### Instructions for using Form No. 21-VDS

**(1)** State the name of the People's Court opening the hearing for resolution of civil matter; if it is a district-level People's Court, urban district People's Court, town People's Court, or provincial city People's Court, state clearly which district-level People's Court, urban district People's Court, town People's Court, or provincial city People's Court (for example: People's Court of Thường Tín District, Hanoi City); if it is a provincial-level People's Court or centrally governed city People's Court, state that provincial (city) People's Court (for example: People's Court of Hà Nam Province).

**(2)** State the number, symbol, date of docketing of the civil matter and the type of civil matter that the People's Court has docketed for resolution.

**(3)** If the civil matter is resolved by one Judge, this content need not be stated.

**(4)** State the full name and title of the Hearing clerk.

**(5)** If the petitioner is an individual, state the full name and address of place of residence and place of work (if any) of that person; if a minor, after the name additionally state the date, month, and year of birth; if an agency or organization, state the name and address of that agency or organization.

**(6)** State only when there is a legal representative of the petitioner. State the full name and address of place of residence and place of work (if any) of that person; state clearly whether the person is a legal representative or an authorized representative of the petitioner; if a legal representative, note in parentheses the relationship between that person and the petitioner; if an authorized representative, note in parentheses: "authorization document dated... month... year...".
Example 1: Mr. Nguyễn Văn A, address... is the legal representative of the petitioner (Director of Thắng Lợi Limited Liability Company).
Example 2: Ms. Lê Thị B, address... is the authorized representative of the petitioner (Authorization document dated... month... year...).

**(7)** State only when there is a guardian of lawful rights and interests of the petitioner. State the full name and address of place of residence and place of work (if any) of that person (if a lawyer, state which Law Firm the lawyer belongs to and which Bar Association); if there are multiple petitioners, state specifically for which petitioner lawful rights and interests are protected.

**(8)** State similarly as guided at point (5).

**(9)** State similarly as guided at point (6).

**(10)** State similarly as guided at point (7).

**(11)** State the full name and address of the witness (if any).

**(12)** State the full name and address of place of work of the interpreter (if any). If there is no place of work, state the address of place of residence.

**(13)** State the full name and address of place of work of the Expert Examiner (if any). If there is no place of work, state the address of place of residence.

**(14)** Must state clearly when a person participating in the hearing is absent from the hearing (and the case does not require adjournment of the hearing), the Presiding Officer of the Hearing must ask whether anyone requests adjournment of the hearing; if there is a request, the Council shall consider and decide to accept or not accept; if not accepted, state clearly the reason and record the decision of the Council.

**(15)** State the questions and answers of persons participating in the hearing, guardians of lawful rights and interests of persons participating in the hearing, or their legal representatives.

**(16)** State amendments and supplements pursuant to requests of the Procurator and persons participating in the hearing: issues recorded in the minutes of hearing that were requested to be amended or supplemented and the specific amendments and supplements; if multiple persons requested amendments and supplements, state each person in order; the person requesting amendments and supplements must sign to confirm.
