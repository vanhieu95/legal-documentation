### Instructions for using Form No. 30-VDS

**(1)** Enter the name of the Court issuing the search notice decision; if it is a district, urban district, town, or provincial city People's Court, specify the name of that district, urban district, town, or provincial city People's Court (for example: People's Court of Thuong Tin District, Hanoi City); if it is a provincial or centrally run city-level People's Court, enter that provincial (city) People's Court (for example: People's Court of Ha Nam Province).

**(2)** The first box records the number and the second box records the year of issuance of the decision (for example: No.: 02/2018/QĐ-TA).

**(3)** Depending on each case, enter the corresponding articles of the Civil Code.

**(4)** The first box records the number and the second box records the year of docketing of the civil matter.

**(5)** If the person submitting the petition requesting declaration of a person as dead is an individual, enter that person's full name and address of residence and place of work (if any); if an agency or organization, enter the name and headquarters address of that agency or organization (as stated in the petition).

**(6)** Enter the full name and date of birth or age of the person requested to be declared dead.

**(7), (8), (9) and (11)** Enter the full name of the person requested to be declared dead.

**(10)** Enter the name and headquarters address of the People's Committee where the person requested to be declared dead resided before disappearance.

**(12)** If a Party has a legal representative, enter the full name of the legal representative.
