### Instructions for using Form No. 15-VDS

**(1)** State the name of the People's Court issuing the decision; if it is a district-level People's Court, urban district People's Court, town People's Court, or provincial city People's Court, state clearly which district-level People's Court, urban district People's Court, town People's Court, or provincial city People's Court (For example: People's Court of Thường Tín District, Hanoi City); if it is a provincial-level People's Court or centrally governed city People's Court, state that provincial (city) People's Court (for example: People's Court of Hanoi City).

**(2)** The first box states the number, the second box states the year, the third box states the symbol of the type of decision (for example: "No.: 20/2018/QĐST-HNGĐ").

**(3)** State the number, year, symbol, and date of docketing of the civil matter.

**(4)** State the type of civil matter that the People's Court has docketed for resolution.

**(5)** If the civil matter is resolved by one Judge, this content need not be stated.

**(6)** State the full name and title of the Hearing clerk.

**(7)** If the petitioner is an individual, state the full name and address of place of residence and place of work (if any) of that person; if it is an agency or organization, state the name and headquarters address of that agency or organization (as stated in the petition).

**(8)** State only when there is a legal representative of the petitioner. State the full name and address of place of residence and place of work (if any); state clearly whether the person is a legal representative or an authorized representative of the petitioner; if a legal representative, note in parentheses the relationship between that person and the petitioner; if an authorized representative, note in parentheses: "authorization document dated... month... year...".
Example 1: Mr. Nguyễn Văn A, address... is the legal representative of the petitioner (Director of Thắng Lợi Limited Liability Company).
Example 2: Ms. Lê Thị B, address... is the authorized representative of the petitioner (Authorization document dated... month... year...).

**(9)** State only when there is a guardian of lawful rights and interests of the petitioner. State the full name and address of place of residence and place of work (if any) (if a lawyer, state which Law Firm the lawyer belongs to and which Bar Association); if there are multiple petitioners, state specifically for which petitioner lawful rights and interests are protected.

**(10)** State similarly as guided at point (7).

**(11)** State similarly as guided at point (8).

**(12)** State similarly as guided at point (9).

**(13)** State the full name and address of other procedural participants (if any).
