### Instructions for using Form No. 18-VDS

**(1)** State the name of the People's Court issuing the decision; if it is a district-level People's Court, urban district People's Court, town People's Court, or provincial city People's Court, state clearly which district-level People's Court, urban district People's Court, town People's Court, or provincial city People's Court (For example: People's Court of Thường Tín District, Hanoi City); if it is a provincial-level People's Court or centrally governed city People's Court, state that provincial (city) People's Court (for example: People's Court of Hà Nam Province).

**(2)** The first box states the number, the second box states the year of the decision (for example: No.: 01/2018/QĐ-TA).

**(3)** If changing the procedural conducting officer at the first-instance stage, state "first-instance resolution of civil matter"; if at the appellate stage, state "appellate resolution of civil matter".

**(4)** State the full name and title of the Hearing clerk.

**(5)** State the reason for changing the procedural conducting officer in accordance with the provisions of the Civil Procedure Code.

**(6) and (7)** State the name of the Judge; in case of changing the Hearing clerk, state the name of the Hearing clerk.

**(8)** State the number, symbol, date of docketing of the civil matter and the type of civil matter that the People's Court has docketed for resolution.

**(9)** If the party has a legal representative, state the name of the legal representative of the party.
