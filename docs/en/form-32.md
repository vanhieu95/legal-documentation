### Instructions for using Form No. 32-VDS

**(1)** Enter the name of the Court issuing the decision; if it is a district, urban district, town, or provincial city People's Court, specify the name of that district, urban district, town, or provincial city People's Court (for example: People's Court of Thuong Tin District, Hanoi City); if it is a provincial or centrally run city-level People's Court, enter that provincial (city) People's Court (for example: People's Court of Ha Nam Province).

**(2)** The first box records the number and the second box records the year of issuance of the decision.

**(3)** Enter the full name and title of the hearing clerk.

**(4)** Enter the venue where the hearing takes place (for example: headquarters of People's Court of Y Yen District, Nam Dinh Province).

**(5) and (15)** Enter the number, symbol, and date of docketing of the civil matter.

**(6)** If the petitioner is an individual, enter that person's full name and address of residence and place of work (if any); if a minor, after the full name also enter the date, month, and year of birth; if an agency or organization, enter the name and address of that agency or organization.

**(7)** Enter only when there is a legal representative of the petitioner. Enter the full name and address of residence and place of work (if any) of that person; clearly state whether the person is a legal representative or an authorized representative of the petitioner; if a legal representative, note in parentheses the relationship between that person and the petitioner; if an authorized representative, note in parentheses: "authorization document dated…. month…. year…..".
Example 1: Mr. Nguyen Van A, address is the legal representative of the petitioner (Director of Thang Loi LLC).
Example 2: Ms. Le Thi B, address ………is the authorized representative of the petitioner (Authorization document dated... month... year...).

**(8)** Enter only when there is a guardian of the lawful rights and interests of the petitioner. Enter the full name and address of residence and place of work (if any) of that person (if a Lawyer, state which law firm the Lawyer belongs to and which Bar Association the Lawyer is a member of); if there are multiple petitioners, specify for which petitioner the lawful rights and interests are being protected.

**(9)** Enter similarly to the instructions at point (6).

**(10)** Enter similarly to the instructions at point (7).

**(11)** Enter similarly to the instructions at point (8).

**(12)** Enter a summary of the content that the petitioner requests the Court to resolve.

**(13)** Enter the findings of the Court and the bases for accepting the petition. In this section, paragraphs are numbered in order within [].

**(14)** Clearly state the corresponding point, clause, and article of the Civil Procedure Code and related laws (if any) serving as the basis for the decision.

**(16)** Enter the content of the agreement of the Parties in the Minutes of successful out-of-court mediation.
