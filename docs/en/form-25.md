### Instructions for using Form No. 25-VDS

**(1)** Enter the name of the Court opening the appellate hearing; if it is a provincial or centrally run city-level People's Court, specify that provincial (city) People's Court (for example: People's Court of Phu Tho Province); if it is a High People's Court, specify where the High People's Court is located (for example: High People's Court in Hanoi).

**(2)** Enter the number, symbol, and date of docketing of the civil matter and the type of civil matter that the Court has docketed for resolution.

**(3)** Enter the full name and title of the hearing clerk.

**(4)** If the petitioner is an individual, enter that person's full name and address of residence and place of work (if any); if a minor, after the full name also enter the date, month, and year of birth; if an agency or organization, enter the name and address of that agency or organization.

**(5)** Enter only when there is a legal representative of the petitioner. Enter the full name and address of residence and place of work (if any) of that person; clearly state whether the person is a legal representative or an authorized representative of the petitioner; if a legal representative, note in parentheses the relationship between that person and the petitioner; if an authorized representative, note in parentheses: "authorization document dated... month... year...".
Example 1 : Mr. Nguyen Van A, address... is the legal representative of the petitioner (Director of Thang Loi LLC).
Example 2: Ms. Le Thi B, address... is the authorized representative of the petitioner (Authorization document dated... month... year...).

**(6)** Enter only when there is a guardian of the lawful rights and interests of the petitioner. Enter the full name and address of residence and place of work (if any) of that person (if a Lawyer, state which law firm the Lawyer belongs to and which Bar Association the Lawyer is a member of); if there are multiple petitioners, specify for which petitioner the lawful rights and interests are being protected.

**(7)** Enter similarly to the instructions at point (4).

**(8)** Enter similarly to the instructions at point (5).

**(9)** Enter similarly to the instructions at point (6).

**(10)** Enter the full name and address of the witness (if any).

**(11)** Enter the full name and address of place of work of the interpreter (if any). If there is no place of work, enter the address of residence.

**(12)** Enter the full name and address of place of work of the expert examiner (if any). If there is no place of work, enter the address of residence.

**(13)** Clearly state the case when a participant is absent from the hearing (and the case is not one requiring adjournment of the hearing), the Presiding Judge of the hearing must ask whether anyone requests adjournment of the hearing; if there is a request, the trial council considers and decides to accept or not accept; if not accepted, clearly state the reason and record the decision of the trial council.

**(14)** Enter the questions and answers of participants in the hearing, guardians of the lawful rights and interests of participants in the hearing, or their legal representatives.

**(15)** Enter amendments and supplements at the request of the Procurator and participants after the hearing concludes; if multiple persons request amendments and supplements, enter them in order for each person. The person requesting amendments and supplements must sign for confirmation.
