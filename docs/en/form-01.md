### Instructions for using Form No. 01-VDS: 

**(1)** State the type of civil matter that the requesting party requests the Court to resolve in accordance with the provisions of the Civil Procedure Code (for example: Request to declare a person missing; request to annul an unlawful marriage; request to annul a resolution of the General Meeting of Shareholders; request to declare a labor contract void;...).

**(2) and (5)** State the name of the Court with jurisdiction to resolve the civil matter; if it is a district-level People's Court, provincial city People's Court, or town People's Court, clearly state which district-level, provincial city, or town People's Court of which province or centrally run city (for example: Thuong Tin District People's Court, Hanoi City); if it is a provincial or centrally run city People's Court, state that provincial (city) People's Court (for example: Ha Nam Provincial People's Court).

**(3)** If the requesting party is an individual, clearly state full name, date of birth, and national identity card/citizen identification card/passport number or other identity document of that person; if it is an agency or organization, state the name of the agency or organization and the full name of the legal representative of that agency or organization. If it is a representative by operation of law, after the full name write "- is the representative by operation of law of the person with the right to request" and clearly state the full name of the person with the right to request; if it is an authorized representative, write "- is the authorized representative of the person with the right to request under the power of attorney established on ………" and clearly state the full name of the person with the right to request. Where multiple persons jointly file the petition, number them 1, 2, 3,... and provide full information for each person.

**(4)** If the requesting party is an individual, state the full address of place of residence and place of work (if any) of that person at the time of filing the petition (for example: Binh An Hamlet, Phu Cuong Commune, My Duc District, Hanoi City); if it is an agency or organization, state the headquarters address of that agency or organization at the time of filing the petition (for example: headquarters at No. 20 Ly Thuong Kiet Street, Hoan Kiem District, Hanoi City).

**(6)** State specifically the contents that the requesting party requests the Court to resolve.

**(7)** Clearly state the reasons, purposes, and grounds for requesting the Court to resolve that civil matter.

**(8)** Clearly state the full names and addresses of place of residence and place of work (if any) of persons whom the requesting party considers related to the resolution of that civil matter.

**(9)** State other information that the requesting party considers necessary for the resolution of their request.

**(10)** Clearly state the names of documents and evidence attached to the petition, whether copies or originals, in order 1, 2, 3,… (for example: 1. Copy of Birth Certificate of Mr. Nguyen Van A; 2. Copy of marriage registration certificate of Mr. Tran Van B and Ms. Pham Thi C;....).

**(11)** State the place and time of filing the petition (for example: Hanoi, 08 December 2018; Hung Yen, 18 February 2019).

**(12)** If the requesting party is an individual, that person's signature or fingerprint is required; if it is an agency or organization, the legal representative of that agency or organization must sign, state full name and position, and affix the seal of that agency or organization. Where the requesting party is an enterprise, the use of the seal shall comply with the provisions of the Law on Enterprises. Where multiple persons jointly request, all shall sign and clearly state the full name of each person at the end of the petition.
