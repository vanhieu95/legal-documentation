### Instructions for using Form No. 09-VDS:
**(1)** State the name of the Court issuing the docketing notice; if it is a district-level People's Court, provincial city People's Court, or town People's Court, clearly state which district-level, provincial city, or town People's Court of which province or centrally run city (for example: Thuong Tin District People's Court, Hanoi City); if it is a provincial or centrally run city People's Court, state that provincial (city) People's Court (for example: Ha Nam Provincial People's Court).

**(2) and (3)** State the name of the requesting party and persons with related rights and obligations; if an individual, state full name and address of place of residence and place of work (if any) of that person; if an agency or organization, state the name and headquarters address of that agency or organization (as stated in the petition). Where sent to a representative by operation of law of the person with the right to request or persons with related rights and obligations, after the name of the person to whom it is sent write "- is the representative by operation of law of the person with the right to request" or "is the representative by operation of law of the person with related rights and obligations" and clearly state the full name of the person with the right to request or the person with related rights and obligations; if sent to an authorized representative, write "- is the authorized representative of the person with the right to request under the power of attorney established on……." or "is the authorized representative of the person with related rights and obligations under the power of attorney established on…….." and clearly state the full name of the person with the right to request or the person with related rights and obligations.

**(4)** The first box states the number, the second box states the year, the third box states the symbol of the type of matter docketed; if regarding civil matters, write "DS"; if regarding marriage and family, write "HNGĐ"; if regarding business and commerce, write "KDTM"; if regarding labor, write "LĐ" (for example: No. 18/2018/TLST-HNGĐ).

**(5)** State the type of civil matter that the Court dockets for resolution (For example: "declare a person missing"; "terminate adoption",...).

**(6) , (7) and (8)** If the requesting party is an individual, state full name, address of place of residence and place of work (if any) of that person; if an agency or organization, state the name and headquarters address of that agency or organization (as stated in the petition).

**(9)** State specifically the matters that the requesting party for resolution of civil matter requests the Court to resolve.

**(10)** State specifically the names of the documents and evidence submitted by the requesting party for resolution of civil matter attached to the petition.

**(11)** State the name of the person being notified.

**(12)** State the name and address of the Court issuing the docketing notice.
