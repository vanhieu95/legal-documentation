### Instructions for using Form No. 28-VDS

**(1)** Enter the name of the Court issuing the search notice; if it is a district, urban district, town, or provincial city People's Court, specify the name of that district, urban district, town, or provincial city People's Court (for example: People's Court of Thuong Tin District, Hanoi City); if it is a provincial or centrally run city-level People's Court, enter that provincial (city) People's Court (for example; People's Court of Ha Nam Province).

**(2)** The first box records the number and the second box records the year of issuance of the decision (for example: No.: 02/2018/QĐ-TA).

**(3)** Depending on each case, enter the corresponding articles of the Civil Code.

**(4)** The first box records the number and the second box records the year of docketing of the civil matter.

**(5)** If the person submitting the petition requesting notice to search for a person absent at place of residence is an individual, enter that person's full name and address of residence and place of work (if any); if an agency or organization, enter the name and headquarters address of that agency or organization (as stated in the petition).

**(6)** Enter the full name and date of birth or age of the person for whom notice to search for absence at place of residence is required.

**(7), (8), (9) and (12)** Enter the full name of the person absent at place of residence.

**(10)** Enter the name and headquarters address of the People's Committee where the person subject to the search notice resided before disappearance.

**(11)** If there is a request to apply measures for management of property of the person absent at place of residence and the request is accepted, enter the decision of the Court on assignment of property management, obligations and rights of the property manager as provided in Articles 65, 66, and 67 of the Civil Code. If there is no request to apply measures for management of property of the person absent at place of residence, enter "no request".

**(13)** If the petitioner has a legal representative, enter the full name of the legal representative.
