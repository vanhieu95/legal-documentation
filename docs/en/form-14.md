### Instructions for using Form No. 14-VDS

**(1)** State the name of the People's Court issuing the decision; if it is a district-level People's Court, urban district People's Court, town People's Court, or provincial city People's Court, state clearly which district-level People's Court, urban district People's Court, town People's Court, or provincial city People's Court (for example: People's Court of Thường Tín District, Hanoi City); if it is a provincial-level People's Court or centrally governed city People's Court, state that provincial (city) People's Court (for example: People's Court of Hà Nam Province).

**(2)** The first box states the number, the second box states the year of issuance of the decision (for example: No.: 02/2018/QĐ-TA).

**(3)** If ordering supplementary expert examination or re-expert examination, state: "SUPPLEMENTARY ORDER OF EXPERT EXAMINATION" or "RE-ORDER OF EXPERT EXAMINATION"

**(4)** Depending on each case, state the corresponding article of the Law on Judicial Expert Examination.

**(5)** State the number, symbol, date of docketing of the civil matter and the type of civil matter that the People's Court has docketed for resolution.

**(6)** Depending on each specific case, state the request of the petitioner or what the People's Court deems necessary. If the petitioner is an individual, state clearly the full name and address of place of residence and place of work (if any) of that person; if it is an agency or organization, state the name and headquarters address of that agency or organization (as stated in the petition).

**(7)** State the subject matter requiring expert examination.

**(8)** State the full name and address of the organization ordered to conduct expert examination or the full name and address of the Expert Examiner ordered to conduct expert examination.

**(9)** State specifically the issues requiring expert examination and the specific requirements for expert examination conclusions.

**(10)** State the names of related documents or comparison samples sent together, specific quantities, symbols (if any).

**(11)** State the deadline within which the judicial expert examination organization or the Expert Examiner ordered to conduct expert examination must send the expert examination conclusion to the People's Court.

**(12)** If the party has a legal representative, state the name of the legal representative of the party.
