### Instructions for using Form No. 10-VDS:
**(1)** State the name of the People's Court issuing the decision; if it is a district-level People's Court, provincial city People's Court, or town People's Court of a province or centrally run city, clearly state which district-level, provincial city, or town People's Court of which province or centrally run city (for example: Thuong Tin District People's Court, Hanoi City); if it is a provincial or centrally run city People's Court, state that provincial (city) People's Court (for example: Ha Nam Provincial People's Court).

**(2)** The first box states the number, the second box states the year of the decision (for example: No.: 01/2018/QĐTĐ).

**(3)** State the number, symbol, day, month, year of docketing of the civil matter and the type of civil matter that the Court dockets for resolution.

**(4)** If the requesting party is an individual, clearly state full name and address of place of residence or place of work of that person; if a minor, after the full name additionally state date, month, year of birth; if an agency or organization, state the name of the agency or organization and the headquarters address of that agency or organization.

**(5)** Where the Chief Justice authorizes the Deputy Chief Justice to sign the decision, write:
