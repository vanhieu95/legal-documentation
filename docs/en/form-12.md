### Instructions for using Form No. 12-VDS

**(1)** State the name of the People's Court issuing the decision; if it is a district-level People's Court, urban district People's Court, town People's Court, or provincial city People's Court, state clearly which district-level People's Court, urban district People's Court, town People's Court, or provincial city People's Court (for example: People's Court of Thường Tín District, Hanoi City); if it is a provincial-level People's Court or centrally governed city People's Court, state that provincial (city) People's Court (for example: People's Court of Hà Nam Province).

**(2)** The first box states the number, the second box states the year of issuance of the decision (for example: No.: 02/2018/QĐ-TA).

**(3)** State the number, symbol, date of docketing of the civil matter and the type of civil matter that the People's Court has docketed for resolution.

**(4)** Depending on each specific case, state the request of the party or what the People's Court deems necessary. In case it is pursuant to a party's request, state clearly the name, address, and party status of the person who submitted the petition.

**(5)** State the full name and address of the individual or the name and address of the agency or organization that the People's Court requests to provide documents and evidence.

**(6) and (8)** State the name of the individual or the name of the agency or organization that the People's Court requests to provide documents and evidence.

**(7)** State specifically the documents and evidence that the People's Court requests to be provided.
