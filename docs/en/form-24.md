### Instructions for using Form No. 24-VDS

**(1)** Enter the name of the Court issuing the decision; if it is a provincial or centrally run city-level People's Court, specify that provincial (city) People's Court (for example: People's Court of Hung Yen Province); if it is a High People's Court, specify which High People's Court (for example: High People's Court in Ho Chi Minh City).

**(2)** The first box records the number, the second box records the year, and the third box records the decision type code (for example: "No.: 20/2018/QĐPT-HNGĐ").

**(3)** Enter the full name and title of the hearing clerk.

**(4)** Enter the number, symbol, and date of docketing of the civil matter and the type of civil matter that the Court has docketed for resolution.

**(5)** If the petitioner is an individual, enter that person's full name and address of residence and place of work (if any); if a minor, after the full name also enter the date, month, and year of birth; if an agency or organization, enter the name and address of that agency or organization.

**(6)** Enter only when there is a legal representative of the petitioner. Enter the full name and address of residence and place of work (if any) of that person; clearly state whether the person is a legal representative or an authorized representative of the petitioner; if a legal representative, note in parentheses the relationship between that person and the petitioner; if an authorized representative, note in parentheses: "authorization document dated... month... year...".
Example 1: Mr. Nguyen Van A, address... is the legal representative of the petitioner (Director of Thang Loi LLC).
Example 2: Ms. Le Thi B, address... is the authorized representative of the petitioner (Authorization document dated... month... year...).

**(7)** Enter only when there is a guardian of the lawful rights and interests of the petitioner. Enter the full name and address of residence and place of work (if any) of that person (if a Lawyer, state which law firm the Lawyer belongs to and which Bar Association the Lawyer is a member of); if there are multiple petitioners, specify for which petitioner the lawful rights and interests are being protected.

**(8)** Enter similarly to the instructions at point (5).

**(9)** Enter similarly to the instructions at point (6).

**(10)** Enter similarly to the instructions at point (7).

**(11)** Clearly state the reason for adjourning the appellate hearing for resolution of civil matter in the specific case provided for in Article 374 of the Civil Procedure Code (for example: Considering that the appellant was lawfully summoned by the Court but was absent from the first hearing due to a work accident requiring emergency treatment at a hospital,...).

**(12)** Depending on each specific case, enter the corresponding point, clause, and article of the Civil Procedure Code.

**(13)** Enter the number, symbol, and date of docketing of the civil matter.

**(14)** Clearly state the time and venue for reopening the appellate hearing for resolution of civil matter (for example: The appellate hearing for resolution of civil matter will be reopened at 08:00 on 10 September 2018 at the headquarters of People's Court of H Province; address No. ...., K Street, X Town, H Province). If the time and venue cannot yet be set, enter "The time and venue for reopening the appellate hearing for resolution of civil matter will be notified by the Court later".

**(15)** If a Party has a legal representative, enter the full name of the legal representative of the Party.
