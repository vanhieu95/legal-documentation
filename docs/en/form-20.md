### Instructions for using Form No. 20-VDS

**(1)** State the name of the People's Court issuing the decision; if it is a district-level People's Court, urban district People's Court, town People's Court, or provincial city People's Court, state clearly which district-level People's Court, urban district People's Court, town People's Court, or provincial city People's Court (For example: People's Court of Thường Tín District, Hanoi City); if it is a provincial-level People's Court or centrally governed city People's Court, state that provincial (city) People's Court (for example: People's Court of Hà Nam Province).

**(2)** The first box states the number, the second box states the year, the third box states the symbol of the type of decision (for example: "No.: 20/2018/QĐST-LĐ").

**(3)** If the civil matter is resolved by one Judge, this content need not be stated.

**(4)** State the full name and title of the Hearing clerk.

**(5) and (14)** State the number, symbol, date of docketing of the civil matter and the type of civil matter that the People's Court has docketed for resolution.

**(6)** If the petitioner is an individual, state clearly the full name and address of place of residence and place of work (if any) of that person; if a minor, after the name additionally state the date, month, and year of birth; if an agency or organization, state the name and address of that agency or organization.

**(7)** State only when there is a legal representative of the petitioner. State the full name and address of place of residence and place of work (if any) of that person; state clearly whether the person is a legal representative or an authorized representative of the petitioner; if a legal representative, note in parentheses the relationship between that person and the petitioner; if an authorized representative, note in parentheses: "authorization document dated... month... year...".
Example 1: Mr. Nguyễn Văn A, address... is the legal representative of the petitioner (Director of Thắng Lợi Limited Liability Company).
Example 2: Ms. Lê Thị B, address... is the authorized representative of the petitioner (Authorization document dated... month... year...).

**(8)** State only when there is a guardian of lawful rights and interests of the petitioner. State the full name and address of place of residence and place of work (if any) of that person (if a lawyer, state which Law Firm the lawyer belongs to and which Bar Association); if there are multiple petitioners, state specifically for which petitioner lawful rights and interests are protected.

**(9)** State similarly as guided at point (6).

**(10)** State similarly as guided at point (7).

**(11)** State similarly as guided at point (8).

**(12)** State specifically the case of suspending resolution of the civil matter (for example: In case of suspension pursuant to the provisions at Clause 2 of Article 367 of the Civil Procedure Code, state: Having considered that the petitioner was lawfully summoned for the second time but was still absent).

**(13)** Depending on each case, state the corresponding article of the Civil Procedure Code to issue the decision.

**(15)** Depending on each case, state the consequences of suspending resolution of the civil matter in accordance with the provisions of the Civil Procedure Code (including advance court fees and other procedural costs).

**(16)** In case there is a person with related rights and obligations, state "person with related rights and obligations".

**(17)** If the party has a legal representative, state the name of the legal representative of the party.

**(18)** In case the civil matter is resolved by one Judge, state:
