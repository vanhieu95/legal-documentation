### Instructions for using Form No. 27-VDS

**(1)** Enter the name of the Court issuing the decision; if it is a provincial or centrally run city-level People's Court, specify that provincial (city) People's Court (for example: People's Court of Hung Yen Province); if it is a High People's Court, specify which High People's Court (for example: High People's Court in Ho Chi Minh City).

**(2)** The first box records the number, the second box records the year, and the third box records the decision type code (for example "No.: 10/2018/QĐPT-LĐ").

**(3)** Enter the type of civil matter that the Court resolves (for example: "Declaration of a person as missing", "Declaration of a person as having lost civil act capacity",...)

**(4)** Enter the full name and title of the hearing clerk.

**(5)** Enter the number, symbol, and date of docketing of the civil matter and the type of civil matter that the Court has docketed for resolution.

**(6)** Enter the name, number, symbol, and date of the Decision that was appealed or protested.

**(7)** If the petitioner is an individual, enter that person's full name and address of residence and place of work (if any); if a minor, after the full name also enter the date, month, and year of birth of that person; if an agency or organization, enter the name and headquarters address of that agency or organization.

**(8)** Enter only when there is a legal representative of the petitioner and enter the full name and address of residence and place of work (if any) of that person; clearly state whether the person is a legal representative or an authorized representative of the petitioner; if a legal representative, note in parentheses the relationship between that person and the petitioner; if an authorized representative, note in parentheses "authorization document dated.... month.... year…..".
Example 1: Mr. Nguyen Van A, residing at... is the legal representative of the petitioner (Director of Thang Loi LLC).
Example 2: Ms. Le Thi B, residing at... is the authorized representative of the petitioner (Authorization document dated... month... year...).

**(9)** Enter only when there is a guardian of the lawful rights and interests of the petitioner. Enter the full name and address of residence and place of work (if any) of that person (if a Lawyer, state which law firm the Lawyer belongs to and which Bar Association the Lawyer is a member of); if there are multiple petitioners, specify for which petitioner the lawful rights and interests are being protected.

**(10)** Enter similarly to the instructions at point (7).

**(11)** Enter similarly to the instructions at point (8).

**(12)** Enter similarly to the instructions at point (9).

**(13)** Enter the full name and procedural capacity of the appellant.

**(14)** Enter the number and date of the protest decision and the person with authority to protest (for example: Protest decision No. ..../….. /…… dated ... month ... year .... of the Chief Procurator of the People's Procuracy of Hanoi City...).

**(15)** Enter a summary of the content of the civil matter, the decision of the first-instance court; the content and reasons for the appeal and protest.

**(16)** Enter the findings of the appellate council regarding the appeal and protest and the bases for accepting or not accepting the appeal and protest. In this section, paragraphs are numbered in order within [].

**(17)** Clearly state the legal bases for issuing the decision.

**(18)** Enter the rulings of the appellate council for resolution of civil matter.

**(19)** Clearly state the persons who must pay appellate court fees for resolution of civil matter and the court fee amount each person must pay; if the case is one not required to pay (or exempted from or reduced appellate court fees), clearly state that appellate court fees are not required to be paid (or are exempted from or reduced).
